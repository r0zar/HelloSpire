The Alchemist

68 HPtoxic green #6ad48aBrew / Distill / Rendernothing sits still
Playing: Idle / gait

Stance

Facing right, hunched forward over an invisible workbench — spine curved, neck craning, head tilted down as if mid-inspection. Knees stay bent throughout; the narrow, coiled stance trades stability for readiness to move. The far hand holds a vial up in front of the face, to the light; the near hand rests by the belt of vials. The satchel hangs from the top of the back, behind everything else.

Movement

Idle is the busiest of the three, deliberately — the raised hand rotates as if turning a vial in the light, the head tilts and re-tilts, and the satchel swings with every shift of weight. Attack is a flask toss: a big, loose overhand wind-up and throw, less precise than the other two characters' attacks, ending in an off-balance wobble that matches this character's wiry, unstable identity. Self buff is Brewing: the vial hand raises high, the head tilts back to watch it, and it holds there fizzing before settling — the character's one moment of stillness, and it's aimed at something they made.

Component map — the grand vision

A structural prototype, not a game asset — same spirit as concept-art/. Real combat rigs are generated by the external sts2-reskin-pipeline workbench into spine/alchemist/; this is a design reference for that pipeline's edits.json (stance/posture/proportions) and its art_in/ (part art), not a replacement for either. See design/alchemist.md for the mechanical identity this stance is built from. The body is skinned with painted cut-out parts in assets/alchemist/, generated by ../image_gen_pipeline/generate_wireframe_parts.py from art_reference/alchemist/ — "Hide art overlay" shows the bare skeleton underneath. Note: the rig colour here (#6ad48a) matches the current code constant, though concept-art/README.md flags it as a likely mismatch against the character's actual intended brass/ember/verdigris palette.
