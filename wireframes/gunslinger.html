The Gunslinger

72 HPweathered brass #d9a05bthe Cylindercalm / dangerous
Playing: Idle / gait

Stance

Contrapposto in profile, facing right toward the enemy side of the screen — a duelist's stance, not a soldier's. Weight sits on the back leg; the front leg is soft, ready to shift. The gun hand is the back hand: elbow cocked behind the body, fingers hovering just above the holstered grip at the waist, about to draw. The off hand (near side) hangs loose, and the duster hangs from the top of the back, behind everything else.

Movement

Idle is mostly still — a slow, low-amplitude sway plus a long coat-tail flutter and a small fast tremor in the gun-hand forearm, the one part of this character that hasn't fully decided to stay calm. Attack is fast and snappy: the revolver clears the holster and the gun hand whips up into aim and kicks back on the shot in under half a second, no wind-up — this class doesn't telegraph. Self buff is a confident cylinder spin: the gun hand rotates through a full flourish while the body leans back a beat, a small showman's gesture between exchanges.

Component map — the grand vision

A structural prototype, not a game asset — same spirit as concept-art/. Real combat rigs are generated by the external sts2-reskin-pipeline workbench into spine/gunslinger/; this is a design reference for that pipeline's edits.json (stance/posture/proportions) and its art_in/ (part art), not a replacement for either. See design/gunslinger.md for the mechanical identity this stance is built from. The body is skinned with painted cut-out parts in assets/gunslinger/, generated by ../image_gen_pipeline/generate_wireframe_parts.py from the character-select art — "Hide art overlay" shows the bare skeleton underneath. The revolver is drawn in the holster at idle and in the hand while a move plays.
