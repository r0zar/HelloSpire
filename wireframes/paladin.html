The Paladin

75 HPgold #e8c46aSeals / Judgmentco-op class
Playing: Idle / gait

Stance

Facing right, toward the enemy side of the screen, feet planted in a wide split, knees almost locked — a stance built to not be moved. Spine dead straight, chest up. The shield arm (far side) is drawn in tight and bent hard, holding the shield out front at guard height; the weapon arm (near side) hangs nearly straight, mace angled forward, relaxed but ready. The cape hangs from the top of the back, behind everything. Nothing about the silhouette suggests retreat — this is the class the design doc calls "durable but slow to kill."

Movement

Idle is a slow, heavy breathing sway (~7s cycle) through the spine, head, and both shoulders — armor moving as one mass — layered with a slower shield-arm "readiness" flex (~20s cycle). Attack is a two-handed mace swing: a wind-up that draws the weapon arm back behind the shoulder and up, a hard strike with torso torque, then settle. Self buff is Judgment: the shield raises and presents, the head tilts up, and the chest sigil pulses — the Seals firing. Both return to idle automatically; the legs and hips keep their own idle sway underneath either one, since only the bones each move actually poses stop breathing on their own.
