The Wanderer

The series' gunslinger reads less like a quick-draw archetype and more like the last practitioner of a nearly extinct discipline — gun-work treated with the same ritual weight as knighthood, carried by a man who has already outlived almost everyone he swore that discipline to. He's defined by a world that has run down around him and a horizon he hasn't stopped walking toward. Source of the sun-bleached duster, the twin low-slung holsters with warm wood grips, the wide hat shadowing the face, and the sense that the walk itself is the point — not whatever's waiting at the end of it.
