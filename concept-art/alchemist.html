Three References, One Formula

Mood Sources
Talisman — The Alchemist

The Ritualist

Reads as a spellcaster first, a fighter a distant second — robes, a satchel, the quiet certainty that every problem has a correct ingredient. Source of the notebook, the belt-worn vials, and the general air of "I have prepared for this."

Dota 2 — Alchemist

The Industrialist

Unapologetically mercenary — a tank of chemical goo strapped to his back, hands stained from handling it, always one eye on the gold count. Source of the backpack apparatus, the acid-burned gloves, and the Gold-as-a-resource instinct.

Fullmetal Alchemist

The Precisionist

State Alchemists read as lean and built for movement, not bulk — the transmutation circle does the visual heavy lifting instead of armor plating. Source of the fitted coat silhouette and the circle underfoot.

Gear Breakdown
01

Portable Alembic

The brass-and-glass rig on their back isn't decoration — it's the starter relic, brewing a fresh Volatile Potion into the belt at the start of every fight.

02

Potion Bandolier

Vials worn across the chest, not tucked in a bag. Every slot on this strap is a decision that's already half-made before the turn starts.

03

Brass Field Goggles

Pushed up, never down. This Alchemist trusts their hands more than their eyes for the delicate work.

04

Acid-Burned Gloves

Every good formula leaves a mark. These have survived more of them than most, and they're not being replaced.

05

Field Notebook & Ledger

Half recipe book, half account book. The margins are where Gold spent and Gold owed get reconciled after every fight.
