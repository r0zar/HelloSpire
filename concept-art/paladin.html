Two References, One Oath

Mood Sources
World of Warcraft — Paladin

The Icon

Heavy, ornate plate built to be recognized from across a battlefield — winged pauldrons, a crested tabard worn like a uniform, and a warm golden-white Light that reads as armor's opposite: soft where the steel is hard. Source of the silhouette, the heraldry, and the glow.

Dungeons & Dragons — Paladin

The Oath

Defined less by gear than by a promise spoken once and kept. The holy symbol worn openly at the throat, the shield raised for someone else before it's raised for you, a blade that turns briefly sacred when the oath demands it. Source of the pendant, the raised smiting blade, and the shield-first stance.

Panoply

Arms & Vestments
01

Sunward Crest

Worn over the armor, not stitched into it — replaceable, because the Vigil outlasts any one knight wearing its colors.

02

Winged Pauldrons

Not a blacksmith's idea of protection. Wings, deliberately — the kind of detail an Order adds so its knights are recognized from across a field.

03

Holy Symbol

The one piece of the panoply that isn't armor at all. Everything else can be replaced; this can't.

04

Vigil Shield

Raised first, always. A Paladin's shield arm tells you more about them than their sword arm does.

05

Radiant Blade

The steel does the cutting. The Light is what makes the wound remembered.
